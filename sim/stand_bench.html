Rigid-sphere HRTF (Brown–Duda) in full 3D, Tikhonov-regularised 2×2 inverse, stands 300 mm either side of the camera at speaker height, servos slewing at 120°/s on both axes. The tracker sees the head Track lag late; Lead aims ahead by extrapolating each ear's velocity. Backlash is gear play the servo must take up before the speaker turns. Dropout drops detection frames, and the tracker holds its last estimate. Speakers radiate as a 50 mm piston: the cone is the −3 dB coverage at 6 kHz, and off-axis treble loss is included in the acoustics. The desk is a surface 160 mm below the speaker axis reflecting 60 %; the filters assume free field, so the reflection caps separation for every curve. With two listeners, crosstalk cancellation turns off and the speakers time-align instead. 5.1 and 7.1 place stands at the ITU / Dolby angles around the seat (fronts ±30°, centre below the camera, 5.1 surrounds ±110°, 7.1 sides ±90° and backs ±150°) with a fixed subwoofer; every speaker aims at its own ear (centre at the head) and is delayed so all arrive together. The true ears are an enrolled profile (153.6 mm apart, 7 mm below and 8 mm behind the generic positions). Two eye points show neither turn nor nod, so "Eyes + offset" drifts in both; "Eyes + seen ear" recovers the head pose once an ear faces the camera (turned 20° or more). Real rooms add reflections that cap separation near 10–20 dB.
